Privacy Policy — Path of Arrows

Effective date: November 12, 2026 Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Path of Arrows (the "Game", bundle ID / package com.lightgame.poa) handles information. The Game is developed and published by the independent developer referred to below as "we", "us", or "our".

In short: the Game does not collect your personal data. It has no account system, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything you do in the Game is stored on your own device and stays there.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or postal address;
  • your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, or location;
  • your device identifiers, advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID), or IP address;
  • your gameplay, progress, scores, or in-app behavior;
  • crash logs, diagnostics, or usage statistics sent from the Game.

The Game contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no attribution SDK, and no social or login SDK. It does not request permission to track you across apps or websites, and it does not display an App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to track.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following to its own private storage on your device so that it can work from one session to the next:

  • level progress, unlocked levels, stars, and best scores;
  • daily-challenge results and your streak;
  • achievement and trophy records;
  • your preferences (sound, music, vibration, guideline overlay, dark mode);
  • whether you have accepted the in-app terms on first launch.

This data never leaves your device. We have no ability to read, copy, or recover it. It is deleted when you delete the Game, and it is included in your device backup only if you have device backups enabled and your platform includes app data in them — in which case the backup is controlled by Apple or Google, not by us.

3. Information you choose to send us

If you tap Feedback in Settings, the Game opens your device's mail app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent until you press send, and you can edit or discard it first. The draft includes the message you write plus basic app and device details (app version, device model, OS version) so we can reproduce a problem.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you attach (for example a screenshot). We use it only to answer you and to fix the issue. Support email is retained for up to 90 days after the conversation ends and then deleted, unless we are required to keep it longer by law.

You are never required to contact us, and you can ask us to delete a support conversation at any time.

4. External links

The Game contains buttons that open pages outside the app:

  • the App Store or Google Play listing (Rate Us);
  • this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service;
  • your mail app (Feedback).

Once a link opens, the destination service's own privacy policy applies. We do not control those services and do not receive information about what you do there.

5. Platform-level data

Apple and Google may collect information about app installs, crashes, and device diagnostics under their own privacy policies. Where you have opted in to share diagnostics with developers, we may receive aggregated, non-identifying crash reports through App Store Connect or Google Play Console. We use these only to diagnose and fix defects. We do not combine them with any other information, and they are not used for advertising or tracking.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because the Game collects no personal information at all, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your privacy rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and the CCPA/CPRA give you rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because we do not collect personal data through the Game, there is normally nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete.

You can erase all Game data yourself at any time by deleting the app (Android: also clear app storage in system settings).

For support emails we hold, you may request access, correction, or deletion by writing to the address in section 9. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Legal bases (GDPR). Where the GDPR applies, we process support emails on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you and, where relevant, your consent given by sending the message. We do not process Game data at all.

8. Data security, retention, and international transfers

The Game stores data only in its own sandbox, protected by your device's operating system and, where enabled, your device passcode and encryption.

We keep support emails only as long as described in section 3. Our email provider may store messages on servers located outside your country. Where required, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards under applicable law.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but the strongest protection here is structural: we do not hold a database of player data to breach.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request under section 7:

We aim to reply within 3 business days.

10. Changes to this policy

If the Game's data practices change — for example if a future version adds online features, advertising, or purchases — we will update this policy, revise the "Last updated" date, and, where required, ask for your consent in the app before the change takes effect. The current version of this policy is always the one linked from the Game's Settings screen and the store listing.
